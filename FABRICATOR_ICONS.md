# Fabricator icons

Prompts for icons that sit in the hotbar, the cargo, and the fabrication bay. Draw them in the same sticker style as `ART_STYLE.md`: top-down or a single chunk, transparent background, dark ink outline, faceted color, one main hue and one light color. No words on the picture. Each icon should read at 48 pixels.

The hover text in the bay is the short version of these notes. The picture is only the object.

## Minerals

### Pale ore
A small cyan crystal chunk, like `mineral.png`. Faceted, brighter on the top faces, a soft cyan glow. It is the common extract from an easy rock.

### Amber ore
The same crystal chunk, recolored amber and gold, with an orange glow. It is the extract from a hard rock.

## Alloys

### Prism glass
A flat hexagonal pane of pale glass, faceted, with a white highlight and a thin cyan edge. Not a rock. It looks manufactured.

### Belt iron
A short grey metal ingot, beveled, with a bright top edge and a darker underside. No rust. It looks like a bar you could hold.

### Amber resin
A rounded drop of amber, faceted like hard candy, with a brighter rim. It should look sticky but still a sticker, not a photo of honey.

### Void filament
A short coil of violet thread, glowing, wrapped once. Dark outline so it does not vanish on the black UI.

## Weapons

### Arc missile
A small cyan dart with a white nose and a short cyan exhaust. Top-down, nose up. One object, not a trail of many missiles.

### Survey flare
A gold flare cartridge, faceted, with a small yellow flame at one end. It counts rocks when used. The icon is the cartridge, not a number.

## Station

### Fabrication bench
A small work table seen from above: a pale metal top, a cyan edge, and one diamond mark. It is the crafting table. The icon should look like a station you carry, not a building.

## Ship parts

### Drive coil
A cyan ring of metal with a brighter inner ring, like a coil seen from above. It raises top speed.

### Hull plate
A blue-grey armor plate, faceted, with a pale edge. Slightly wider than it is tall. It raises maximum hull.

### Math hint
A small gold tile with a single pale diamond mark, no numerals. It is the hint item.

### Layer jumper
A cyan device the size of a coil, with two short prongs and a pale core. It is the part that unlocks layer travel. Do not draw a Q or an E.

## HUD tools

### Layer map
A small rounded rectangle, dark fill, cyan border, with a few tiny rock dots and one white ship dot. This is the bottom-right flight map. Fabricating it turns that map on.

### Layer compass
A pale cyan disc with a single needle pointing up and a small diamond at the center. Worn in the hull, it draws the current layer as a line with the ship marked on it.

### Ore ping
An amber ring with a small cyan crystal in the middle. It reports the distance to the nearest drop.

### Gate marker
A violet diamond frame, hollow, with a soft glow. It reports the distance to the wormhole.

## Abilities

### Repair patch
A green hexagonal patch with a pale cross of light, faceted, not a medical symbol made of thin lines.

### Phase blink
A violet chevron pointing up, with a faint second chevron behind it. It hops the ship forward.

### Tractor pulse
An amber horseshoe magnet shape, faceted, open toward the top, with a small cyan spark in the gap. It pulls nearby drops.

### Quiet running
A grey rounded hull chip with a dim cyan slit, quieter than the other icons: less glow, same outline. It stops bump damage for a few seconds.
