# Roadmap: Mentaris: Prisms of Assembly

Later interface work. This file does not change the current game. Names below are open until chosen.

1. Weapon and item selector dock. A dock on the right side of the screen, not along the bottom. Styled like the macOS Dock, with magnification and no hover: the selected item's icon is enlarged, and the mouse scroll wheel changes the selection, the way a hotbar cycles. Scrolling moves between the asteroid-breaking tool and the gunship (attacks aliens).

2. Cargo hold (collected-items display). Similar in spirit to a full-screen list of what you are carrying, opened by pressing a button. No default key yet. It shows everything the ship has collected from asteroid-breaking, and it sits alongside the dock in item 1. Other names for the same screen: cargo bay, manifest, hold, stowage.

Terminology (open, undecided; do not pick yet):
1. Asteroid-breaking: "extract" or "excavate".
2. Item-making (making an item from gathered material): "assemble", "fabricate", or "manufacture".
