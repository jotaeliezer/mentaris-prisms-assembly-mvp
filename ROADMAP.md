# Roadmap: Mentaris: Prisms of Assembly

Later interface work. This file does not change the current game.

1. Weapon and item selector dock. A dock on the right side of the screen, not along the bottom. Styled like the macOS Dock, with magnification and no hover: the selected item's icon is enlarged, and the mouse scroll wheel changes the selection, the way a hotbar cycles. Scrolling moves between the extractor (breaks asteroids) and the gunship (attacks aliens).

2. Cargo hold (collected-items display). Similar in spirit to a full-screen list of what you are carrying, opened by pressing a button. No default key yet. It shows everything the ship has collected by extracting asteroids, and it sits alongside the dock in item 1. Other names for the same screen: cargo bay, manifest, hold, stowage.

Terminology. Making an item from gathered material is called extract. That is the word for the dock's extractor and for the material the cargo hold lists.
